# Obtain business travel approval before booking

Before booking business travel, submit the trip request through the travel portal with destination, dates, business purpose and cost center. Follow the approval route shown for your team. Use the approved booking channel once the request is authorized.
Check the applicable travel policy for permitted booking classes, accommodation and changes. Do not assume that a personal booking will be reimbursed or that a travel approval covers every incidental expense. For a cancellation, contact the booking provider through the corporate channel and retain the reference. After travel, submit eligible expenses with receipts through the expense portal. The helpdesk cannot approve travel, issue a visa or guarantee a refund from an airline or hotel.
