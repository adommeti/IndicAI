# Leave policy, balance, applications and cancellation

Open the HR portal and select Leave. The policy tab shows the leave types and eligibility rules applicable to your employment record. The balance tab shows available days, approved future leave, and pending requests. Check the applicable policy before assuming a carry-forward limit or entitlement; the helpdesk cannot infer your balance from a chat.
To apply, select the leave type, dates and duration, add the required reason or supporting document, and submit for manager approval. Track the request in My Applications. To cancel or change a submitted request, open that application and use the available cancellation or amendment action; an approved request may need manager confirmation. If the displayed balance looks wrong, send HR the request reference and dates. Do not share medical details in general chat. Public holidays and team rosters are separate from an individual's leave balance.
