# Find the office holiday calendar

Open the HR portal's Holiday Calendar and select the office location and calendar year associated with your employee record. Check the published list and any local office announcements. Team rosters, shift coverage and optional holidays may require separate manager confirmation.
A holiday shown for one location does not automatically apply to every employee. If your assigned location is incorrect, ask HR to review the record through the approved change process. For a conflict with an on-call roster, discuss coverage with your manager before making travel plans. The helpdesk can locate the calendar but cannot declare a company holiday or approve an absence. Personal leave applications and balances are handled in the Leave section.
