# Request a replacement work laptop

Use the IT portal's laptop replacement form when your assigned work laptop needs replacement. Enter the asset tag or device model, describe the fault or business reason, and attach a photo of physical damage if safe. Submit the request for manager approval. IT checks warranty, repair options, inventory, and eligibility before confirming a replacement or loan device.
For a laptop that will not start, record whether the charging indicator appears and whether any error is displayed. Do not open the casing. Keep company files in approved managed storage; coordinate backup and transfer with IT before returning the old device. A request does not guarantee a new model or an immediate dispatch date. Track approval and allocation in the same IT portal request. If the battery is swollen or overheating, stop using the device and contact IT for safe handling.
