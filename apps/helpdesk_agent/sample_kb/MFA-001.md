# Lost authenticator phone or repeated MFA prompts

If the phone used for company authentication is lost, replaced or unavailable, raise an IT identity-support ticket through an approved alternate contact route. IT must verify identity before enrolling a new authenticator or changing a recovery method. The assistant cannot bypass multi-factor authentication or supply a temporary code.
If you receive an unexpected approval prompt, deny it and report the event to Security. Never approve a prompt simply because a caller says they are from IT. Do not share backup codes or screenshots containing setup secrets. For repeated prompts during a legitimate sign-in, record the application and time, then seek support. Avoid repeatedly retrying if the account appears locked.
