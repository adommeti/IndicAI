# Raise a confidential workplace concern

Use the confidential employee-relations channel listed in the HR portal to report a workplace concern. Provide a factual account, dates and any relevant references through the secure form. Share sensitive evidence only with the authorized case owner, not in a general helpdesk chat.
If the concern involves your usual reporting manager, the policy lists an alternate HR contact. Check the acknowledgement for the case reference and the next contact route. The helpdesk does not investigate allegations, decide findings or promise an outcome. Do not retaliate against anyone involved in a concern. If there is an immediate threat to personal safety, contact local emergency services or on-site Security rather than waiting for a routine ticket response.
