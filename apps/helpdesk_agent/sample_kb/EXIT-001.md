# Employee exit clearance and equipment return

After HR confirms the exit workflow, open the clearance checklist in the HR portal. Follow the assigned tasks for knowledge transfer, outstanding expenses, equipment return and access removal. Coordinate handover dates with your manager and the responsible support teams.
Return company equipment using IT's documented collection process and obtain an acknowledgement with asset details. Do not wipe a laptop, delete business records or copy company files to personal storage unless the approved handover instructions explicitly permit the action. HR or Payroll confirms final settlement status through the secure portal. The helpdesk cannot calculate settlement amounts or promise a release date. Escalate a missing clearance acknowledgement to the team that owns that checklist item.
