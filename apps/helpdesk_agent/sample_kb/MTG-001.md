# Meeting microphone, speaker or camera is not working

In the meeting application's device settings, select the intended microphone, speaker and camera. Run the built-in test before joining a call. Check the headset connection, physical mute switch and camera privacy shutter. Confirm that the operating system permits the application to use the microphone or camera.
Close another application that may be holding the camera, then rejoin the meeting once. Avoid installing unapproved drivers or remote-support software suggested by an unknown participant. If the issue persists, record the device model, affected application and whether it happens in every meeting. Contact IT with those details. For urgent participation, use an approved alternate audio option if your meeting policy allows it.
