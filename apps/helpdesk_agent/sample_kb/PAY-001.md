# Find a payslip or report a salary discrepancy

Sign in to the payroll section of the HR portal and select the relevant pay period to download your payslip. Compare earnings, deductions and reimbursements with the previous period before raising a discrepancy. A changed tax declaration, unpaid absence or a new benefit election can affect the net amount, but Payroll must confirm the actual reason.
Raise a confidential payroll request with the pay period and the affected line item. Avoid attaching a full payslip in a public channel or sharing salary details with unrelated colleagues. Changes to bank account information must use the verified payroll workflow, not a chat instruction. The helpdesk can point you to the right form but cannot amend salary records, guarantee a payment date, or calculate your personal tax liability.
