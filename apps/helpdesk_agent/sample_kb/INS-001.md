# Locate health insurance card and claim contacts

Open Benefits in the HR portal and follow the insurer or administrator link to retrieve your current insurance membership card. Confirm the covered member's name and policy period. The plan's published contact directory provides the appropriate route for claim-status questions, network hospital information and authorization requests.
For an incorrect membership record, contact the benefits team through a confidential request. For a claim dispute, use the insurer's published grievance process and keep the claim reference. Do not upload medical documents in ordinary helpdesk chat. The helpdesk cannot diagnose a condition, approve treatment, determine whether a hospital is covered, or promise reimbursement. In an emergency, seek appropriate emergency assistance rather than waiting for a helpdesk response.
