# Submit tax declarations and investment proof

The payroll portal lists the current declaration window and the evidence accepted for each tax declaration category. Review the instructions for the applicable financial year before entering amounts. Save a draft, check supporting documents for legibility, and submit through the designated proof-upload workflow.
An uploaded document may remain pending until Payroll reviews it. If proof is rejected, read the reviewer comment and correct the specific issue. Do not send identity numbers or investment statements through ordinary helpdesk chat. This sample article does not determine which tax regime is best for an employee and is not tax advice. Ask Payroll about a portal or processing issue; obtain qualified advice for personal tax decisions.
