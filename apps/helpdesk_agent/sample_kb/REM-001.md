# Request a remote-work arrangement

Review the remote-work policy in the HR portal and discuss the proposed arrangement with your manager. Use the designated request form to record the work location, dates, business reason and availability plan. Wait for approval before treating the arrangement as confirmed.
Remote work does not remove information-security requirements. Use the managed laptop, approved storage and required network access tools. Keep confidential calls and screens away from unauthorized viewers. Changes to work location may require additional HR review, particularly when crossing jurisdictions; do not assume that an earlier approval covers a different location. For a pending request, contact the approver shown in the portal. For a technical connectivity problem, raise a separate IT request.
