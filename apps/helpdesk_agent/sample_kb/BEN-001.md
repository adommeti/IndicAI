# View benefits and update dependent information

The Benefits section of the HR portal shows the plans and enrollment options associated with your employee record. Read the plan documents for eligibility, coverage dates and any required evidence. To add or correct a dependent, use the secure benefits change form and submit the documents requested by HR.
Check the enrollment status after submission. An accepted form is not confirmation that an insurer has activated coverage; wait for the official confirmation or updated plan record. Do not share medical records or dependent identity documents in a general chat. Contact the benefits team for a discrepancy or a time-sensitive enrollment event. The helpdesk cannot promise coverage or approve an exception to plan rules.
