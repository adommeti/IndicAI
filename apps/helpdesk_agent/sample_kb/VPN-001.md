# Connect to the company VPN from home

The company VPN gives remote employees access to internal websites and shared applications. First confirm that your home internet works by opening an ordinary public website. Open the managed VPN application installed on your work laptop, choose the company connection, and sign in with your company account. Complete the authenticator challenge when prompted. Wait for the connected indicator before opening an internal resource.
If connection fails, disconnect and reconnect once, confirm the laptop clock is correct, and note the exact error. A working internet connection is required; the VPN cannot fix a broadband outage. Never disable endpoint protection or install a personal VPN to bypass controls. If access still fails, send IT the error message and approximate time, without passwords or one-time codes. An expired company password must go to an IT support ticket; the assistant cannot reset it.
