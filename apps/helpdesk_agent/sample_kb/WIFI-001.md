# Join company office Wi-Fi

On your managed laptop, open the wireless network menu and select the company Wi-Fi network published by office IT. Sign in with your company identity when prompted and verify any certificate prompt against the IT instructions. After connecting, open a browser to confirm internet access. The connected icon alone does not prove that traffic is working.
If company Wi-Fi is unavailable, check that Wi-Fi is enabled and airplane mode is off, then move within range of an office access point. Note the office location and whether colleagues are affected. Do not join a look-alike network or share your credentials with a visitor. Guests must use the approved guest access process. If the device remains unable to connect, contact IT with the network name, device type and error. For an expired company password, request an IT ticket.
