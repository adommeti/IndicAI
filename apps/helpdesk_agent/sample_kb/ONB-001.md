# New employee onboarding checklist

Use the onboarding portal to review the tasks assigned to your joining record. Typical tasks include secure document submission, manager introduction, mandatory training and equipment collection. Complete only the forms assigned to you and track their status in the portal.
If a task is missing or a link is inaccessible, ask the onboarding coordinator to check your employee record. Do not resend identity documents in an email thread simply because a portal task is delayed. IT account provisioning and security access require separate approvals; completion of an HR form does not automatically grant system access. Your manager confirms the first-day schedule and work location. Raise specific requests for a laptop or ID badge through their designated forms.
