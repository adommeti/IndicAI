# Work laptop storage is nearly full

Open the operating system's storage settings to see which categories use space. Remove only personal temporary files that you recognize and no longer need, following the company retention policy. Move business documents to approved managed storage if your team permits it, then confirm that synchronization has completed.
Do not delete system folders, security software, mail data files or shared business records to free space. Avoid unapproved cleanup utilities. If storage remains low, contact IT with the available-space figure and the affected application. IT can assess logs, managed caches or a hardware upgrade. Back up active work through the approved service before any maintenance; the helpdesk cannot recover files that were never saved or backed up.
