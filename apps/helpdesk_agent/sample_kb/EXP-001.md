# Submit and track expense reimbursement

Open the expense portal and create a reimbursement claim. Select the expense category and business purpose, enter the transaction date, amount and currency, and attach legible itemized receipts or invoices. Check that each attachment matches its line item, then submit the claim for the required approvals. Eligibility and submission deadlines are shown in the applicable expense policy; approval is not automatic.
Use My Claims to see whether a claim is a draft, pending approval, returned, approved, or paid. For a rejected or returned reimbursement, open the reviewer comments and rejection reason, correct the requested fields or attachments, and resubmit through the portal. Avoid creating a duplicate claim for the same receipt. For a payment discrepancy, contact Finance with the claim reference and status. Never post bank details, full card numbers, or receipt images containing personal data in a public helpdesk conversation.
