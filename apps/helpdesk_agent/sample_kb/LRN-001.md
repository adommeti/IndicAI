# Access mandatory learning and training records

Open the learning portal from the employee application catalogue and sign in with your company account. My Learning shows assigned courses, due dates and completion status. Use the supported browser listed by the portal and allow the course's required pop-up window.
If a completed course still appears pending, check whether the final assessment and completion screen were submitted. Record the course title and completion time, then contact the learning administrator. Do not repeat a paid external course or purchase a license without approval. Mandatory training deadlines and exemptions are controlled by HR; the helpdesk cannot mark a course complete or grant an exemption. For account access errors, raise an IT support request.
