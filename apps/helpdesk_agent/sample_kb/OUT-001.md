# Outlook email is not syncing or sending

If Outlook is not receiving recent messages or an email remains in the Outbox, first check that the laptop has an internet connection. In Outlook, check that Work Offline is turned off. Use Send/Receive to request a synchronization, then allow time for large attachments to transfer. Confirm that the correct company mailbox is selected.
Check the company webmail page to distinguish a desktop-only problem from a mailbox or service outage. Restart Outlook once if the application is unresponsive. Do not delete your mail profile or local data files without IT guidance. If syncing still fails, collect the displayed error, the last time mail arrived, and whether webmail works. Open an IT support request with those details, excluding message contents and credentials. Password expiry or an account lock must be handled through a support ticket, not a reset performed by the assistant.
